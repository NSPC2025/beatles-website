Date: August 15, 2025

Location: Madison Square Garden, New York City

Time: 8:00 PM

Event Details

The Beatles are coming back for a spectacular live concert experience in New York City! This event celebrates the timeless music and legacy of the band with a specially curated setlist, visual effects, and guest performers. Fans can expect a night full of iconic songs, stories behind the music, and a tribute to their lasting impact on pop culture.

Tickets are available now. Early bird discounts apply until July 1st.

Line-up

  • Paul McCartney (Vocals, Bass)
  • Ringo Starr (Drums, Vocals)
  • John Lennon Tribute by Special Guest
  • George Harrison Tribute by Special Guest

Additional Information

Parking is available on-site. Please arrive at least 1 hour before the event starts for security checks. Food and merchandise booths will be open during the event.
