Formation (1960–1962)

The Beatles were formed in Liverpool in 1960, initially with members John Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison, Stuart Sutcliffe, and Pete Best.

Early Success (1963–1965)

The Beatles rise to fame in the UK and later internationally.

Mid Period (1966–1967)

The Beatles evolve artistically, exploring new musical styles and studio techniques.

Late Period (1968–1970)

The Beatles release their most ambitious works, but internal tensions rise leading to their breakup.

Legacy & Influence

The Beatles remain one of the most influential bands in music history.
