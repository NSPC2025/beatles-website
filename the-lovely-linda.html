Composer: Paul McCartney

Length: 0:45

Recording Date: December 1969

Background

The Lovely Linda was the first track Paul McCartney recorded for his debut solo album. Intended initially as a test of his new home studio setup, it became the whimsical opener to the *McCartney* album.

The song was inspired by Paul’s wife Linda, and its brief, casual nature sets the tone for much of the rest of the album — intimate, homegrown, and spontaneous.

Personnel

  • Paul McCartney – vocals, acoustic guitar, bass, percussion
  • Linda McCartney – backing vocals (uncredited)

Trivia

It fades in with background noise — a deliberate production choice by Paul to emphasize the informal, homemade aesthetic of the album.
